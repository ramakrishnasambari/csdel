Who We Are

SELF is a not-for-profit organisation committed to advancing sustainable development by placing communities at the centre of change. We work at the intersection of people, planet, and progress.


Protect and restore the environment

Promote responsible practices

Create inclusive livelihoods

Our Focus Areas

Sustainability

Encouraging responsible consumption, climate resilience, and long-term community planning to balance economic growth and social well-being.

Environment

Protecting natural resources, strengthening biodiversity, and encouraging climate-positive actions through community participation.

Livelihoods

Empowering women and youth through skill development and access to sustainable income-generation opportunities.

About Us

SELF was established with the belief that true development happens when environmental responsibility and livelihoods grow together.

Our Vision

A sustainable, equitable, and resilient future for all.

Our Mission

To promote sustainability and strengthen livelihoods through community-led initiatives and innovation.
